Milon ("the App", "we", "us") is a social app for sharing photos and videos, following people, and messaging friends. This Privacy Policy explains what information Milon collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

1. Who runs Milon

Milon is developed and operated by [your name or business name], based in [your country]. You can reach us at [your contact email] with any privacy question or request.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • The email address and password you sign up with (handled by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your raw password ourselves)
  • Your display name, username, profile photo, bio, and any YouTube/Facebook links you choose to add

Content you create

  • Photos and videos you post, along with captions, privacy settings, and any sound/tag you attach
  • Comments and replies you write, and likes/saves/reposts you make
  • Direct messages you send — text, photos, videos, and voice notes — stored so the conversation is available to you and the recipient

Social graph

  • Who you follow and who follows you, accounts you've blocked, and posts/people you've pinned

Device permissions

Milon may ask your device for permission to use:

  • Camera and microphone — to record photos, videos, and voice messages you choose to create
  • Photos and videos (gallery) — to let you pick existing media to post or send
  • Notifications — to alert you about messages, likes, comments, and follows

Your device's app-permissions screen may list Contacts and Location as permissions the app can request — Milon does not currently read your contacts or your location for any feature. If that changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first.

Information saved only on your device

When you use "Save video" for offline viewing, a copy of that video is stored locally on your device (not uploaded anywhere new). Clearing your device storage or using "Free up space" / account logout removes it.

3. How we use your information

  • To operate core features: your feed, profile, follows, messaging, likes, comments, and notifications
  • To enforce privacy settings you choose (private account, who can comment/mention/message you)
  • To act on reports — reviewing reported accounts, posts, and comments, and automatically hiding content that receives multiple reports
  • To keep your account secure (password changes, sign-in)

Milon does not show ads, and does not use any third-party advertising or analytics SDK. We do not sell your data to anyone.

4. Who your information is shared with

  • Other users — content you post publicly is visible to whoever your privacy settings allow; direct messages are visible only to you and the recipient
  • Firebase (Google) — our backend infrastructure provider. Firebase stores and processes account, content, and message data on our behalf, under Google's own data-processing terms. We do not use any other third-party data processor.

We do not share your personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party for their own marketing purposes.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete content — you can edit your profile at any time, and delete any post, comment, or message you've sent
  • Block and report — you can block any account, and report accounts, posts, or comments that violate our Terms
  • Private account — switch your account to private so only approved followers see your posts
  • Delete your account — available under Settings → Security & permissions → Delete account. This permanently and irreversibly deletes your account sign-in, your posts (and their media), your comments (including comments you left on other people's posts), your direct message threads, and your notifications — and removes every trace of you from other people's posts (likes, saves, reposts, view history) and from other users' followers, following, blocked, and pending-follow-request lists.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active. If you delete individual posts, comments, or messages, we remove them immediately. Deleting your account (see above) removes your data immediately and permanently — we don't keep a backup copy after that point.

7. Children's privacy

Milon is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children under that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at [your contact email] and we will remove it.

8. Security

We use Firebase's authentication and database security rules to restrict access to your data to your own account and the people you interact with. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.
